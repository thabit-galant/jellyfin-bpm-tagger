BPM Tagger

Detects the tempo of each music track and adds it as a Jellyfin tag, e.g. "BPM:128", so the SmartLists plugin can filter by tempo. Your audio files are not changed. Run it from Dashboard → Scheduled Tasks → Library → Detect BPM of music; by default it also runs nightly at 03:00.

Jellyfin tag

Default "BPM:" matches the MusicTags plugin's format. Tracks with this tag are skipped on later runs.
Off: BPM values already in your files are trusted and simply copied to the Jellyfin tag.

Detection

Results outside this range are doubled or halved into it. The range must span at least an octave (highest ≥ 2 × lowest). Raise the lowest value (e.g. 90–185) if drum & bass and fast rock come out at half speed; keep it low (70–180) so slow hip-hop isn't doubled.
Analysis starts 30 seconds in to skip intros. 60 seconds is a good balance of speed and accuracy.
Tracks without a clear beat (ambient, classical, spoken word) score low and are left untagged.

Performance

Use 1 on a Raspberry Pi or low-power NAS.
Spread a large library over several nights.
